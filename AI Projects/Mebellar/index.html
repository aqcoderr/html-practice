Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our products and services.

How does international white-glove delivery operate?

Our specialized logistics team handles door-to-room placement, complete assembly, and packaging removal anywhere across the globe.

Are your woods and textiles sustainably certified?

Yes. 100% of our timber is FSC-certified, and our textiles meet rigorous OEKO-TEX ecological standards.

Can I request custom fabric swatches?

Certainly. You may order a complimentary curated physical swatch box directly through our contact concierge service.
