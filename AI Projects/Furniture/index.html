Our Story

Made by hand, made to last

AURA LUXE began in a small joinery workshop with a simple belief: furniture should be an inheritance, not a purchase. Every piece still passes through the hands of a master craftsperson before it reaches your door.

Today we work with over 120 artisans across Europe and Central Asia, sourcing solid timber, full-grain leather, and natural fibers — never veneer, never particleboard.

  • Solid materials only — no MDF, no laminate
  • Small-batch production, made to order
  • Carbon-neutral shipping on every order
